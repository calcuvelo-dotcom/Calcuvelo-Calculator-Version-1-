How these guides are written

Each guide starts from a question people actually ask — which debt to pay first, how big a mortgage is sensible, whether to lock money into a GIC — and follows it through with a worked example instead of a rule of thumb alone. Where the answer depends on an assumption (a rate, a tax situation, a time horizon), the guide says so and shows what happens when the assumption changes. Where a result surprised me, such as a debt-payoff order that barely matters or a savings comparison that flips at a lower return, I report that too.
